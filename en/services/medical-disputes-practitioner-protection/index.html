Typical issues · Isu yang sering kami tangani

Situations that usually bring clients to us.

  • Patient complaints not yet handled in a structured way
  • A demand letter (somasi) from a patient or their counsel
  • A summons from a professional disciplinary body
  • Completeness of medical records and informed consent
  • Public communication during press coverage or negative reviews
  • Negotiation and mediation of a settlement
  • Examination by a relevant agency
  • No internal complaint-handling procedure

FAQ · Pertanyaan umum

FAQ · Pertanyaan umum

What should we do when a patient complaint arrives?

Record the facts, secure the documents and avoid replying publicly. Contact us so the next steps can be considered together. Please do not send patient data through open channels.

Do you support individual doctors?

Yes. We can support practitioners individually as well as clinics, taking possible conflicts of interest into account.

Is there any guarantee of outcome?

No. We do not promise any particular result. We help organise the facts and the steps carefully and with proper documentation.

How is confidentiality handled?

Information is handled to professional confidentiality standards. We explain a safe way to share documents after the initial conversation.
