Promotion & Product Compliance

Reviewing promotional and product material before it goes live, so the clinic's message stays informative and within the limits that apply to health services and products.

Last reviewed 18 September 2026

Who it's for · Untuk siapa

Who it's for · Untuk siapa

Clinics and marketing teams

Preparing advertising, a website or a social media content calendar.

Doctors on social media

Building a personal presence without going beyond promotion limits.

Product owners

Marketing care products through clinics or online channels.

Typical issues · Isu yang sering kami tangani

Situations that usually bring clients to us.

  • Outcome claims in promotional material
  • Use of before-and-after photos of procedures
  • Patient testimonials in clinic content
  • Influencer collaborations without agreements or guidelines
  • Products marketed without clarity on their registration status
  • Discount or package promotions for medical services
  • Doctors' personal-account content that reads like advertising
  • No internal content approval process

FAQ · Pertanyaan umum

FAQ · Pertanyaan umum

May a clinic show before-and-after photos?

Restrictions on this need to be checked against the rules that apply. We review your material and explain the limits.

May a doctor promote services on a personal account?

There are limits to observe. We help draft guidelines so content stays educational and within the rules.

What should be checked before marketing a care product?

The product registration status, the claims on the label and the responsible party. We help check each one.

Can material reviews be done regularly?

Yes, through a retainer with an agreed review workflow.
