About · Tentang

A small, focused and careful legal practice.

TIM & Co. was founded for one specific need: aesthetic clinics and medical practitioners who want to practise with peace of mind amid constantly changing regulation.

  • Philosophy

    Prevention before disputes

    Most clinic risk can be managed from the start through the right licences, contracts and procedures.

  • How we speak

    Clear, not complicated

    We explain regulation in language that owners and doctors can apply straight away.

  • Scale

    Small by choice

    A lean team allows direct communication and full attention on every engagement.

Why this field

Aesthetic medicine sits where healthcare, business and promotion meet.

A single clinic can be subject to healthcare facility rules, business licensing, employment, advertising and consumer protection all at once. We chose to go deep on this intersection rather than handle everything in general.

This focus lets us understand how clinics actually operate: from visiting-doctor schedules to social media posts.

Working approach

How we treat every engagement.

  1. 01

    Written scope

    Scope of work, timeline and fees are agreed before work begins.

  2. 02

    Confidentiality from the start

    Clinic and patient information is handled to professional confidentiality standards.

  3. 03

    Grounded in regulation

    Every recommendation comes with the relevant legal basis and its review date.

  4. 04

    Documents you can use

    Our output is documents and SOPs that the clinic team can apply straight away.
