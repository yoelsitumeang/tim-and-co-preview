SIP, STR and SIOK: the licensing order that is often got wrong

Last reviewed 14 September 2026By Titin Hariati Siburian, S.H.1 min read
i

This article is general information, not legal advice, and does not create an advocate–client relationship. Regulations may change after the review date above.

Many new clinics are delayed not because documents are missing, but because they are filed in the wrong order. These three licences depend on one another.

The STR (Surat Tanda Registrasi, registration certificate) proves that a medical professional is registered. The SIP (Surat Izin Praktik, practice licence) is issued for a specific place of practice and requires a valid STR. The clinic’s operating licence, the SIOK (Surat Izin Operasional Klinik), in turn requires medical personnel holding a SIP at that location.

The order we generally suggest

  1. Confirm each doctor’s STR is still valid and matches their competence.
  2. Settle the business entity and the KBLI (business classification code) that match the clinic’s services.
  3. Apply for the SIP for the clinic address, then complete the operating licence.

Mistakes that often occur

A visiting doctor practising without a SIP for the clinic location, or services changing without a licence update, are two issues to check in every Legal Health Check.

Every clinic is different. The type of clinic, its location and its medical staffing all affect the licences required, so the order above should be matched to your situation.
